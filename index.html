    Kaise kaam karta hai? / Tips
    • EPUB: pehle book ke Table of Contents se chapters liye jaate hain. Agar TOC galat ho to “Heading text” mode try karein.
    • TXT: “Chapter 12”, “CHAPTER XII”, “Chapter Twelve”, “Ch. 5”, “अध्याय 5”, “第12章”, “Prologue/Epilogue” jaisi headings auto detect hoti hain.
    • Agar headings sirf numbers hain (“1.”, “002”) to Bare numbers pattern on karein.
    • Koi alag format ho (jaise “Kaand 3”) to custom regex dalein: ^Kaand\s+(\d+)
    • * Kindle ke liye EPUB ko “Send to Kindle” se bhejein.
    • Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter = ZIP download.
