    Questions

    What does this tool do?

    It splits a large novel — for example a 500-chapter EPUB — into smaller files, such as 10 chapters per file.

    Chapters are detected incorrectly, or not at all.

    Use the suggestion buttons at the top of step 2. Files with bare numbers as headings ("1.", "002") are fixed by "Numbered headings". For full control, switch to Advanced and open "Chapter detection settings".

    Can I skip some chapters?

    Yes — untick them in the chapter list in step 2. Unticked chapters are left out of every file.

    How do I read the files on a Kindle?

    Choose EPUB as the output format, then send the files with Amazon's "Send to Kindle" service.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser, and the page keeps working offline once loaded.
