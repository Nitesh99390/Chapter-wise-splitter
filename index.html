    ❓ Help & FAQ
    • What does this app do? It splits a large novel (e.g. a 500-chapter EPUB) into smaller files — for example 10 chapters per file.
    • Chapters detected incorrectly? Use the suggestion buttons at the top of step 2, or switch to Advanced mode and open "Chapter detection settings".
    • No chapters found? Your file may use bare numbers as headings ("1.", "002") — click the "Numbered headings" button.
    • Want to skip some chapters? Untick them in the list in step 2.
    • Reading on Kindle? Choose EPUB, then send the files with Amazon's "Send to Kindle" service.
    • Is my file uploaded anywhere? No. Everything happens inside your browser — it even works offline.
    • Shortcut: Ctrl+Enter = ZIP download.
